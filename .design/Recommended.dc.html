One global control, a legend that states the rule
The lens belongs in Settings because it governs every time-based view. The legend under it carries the semantics, so the words Record, Work and Floor never have to be self-explanatory. Provenance shading is what keeps the Work lens honest: it separates the spans the log measured from the ones we inferred, on the chart, without a caption.
The decision that matters more than the placement
The lens must be applied when the chart is drawn, not when the file is written. Baking a choice into the CSV locks a published transcript to one reading, and the whole argument is that no single reading is sufficient. The converter should emit the facts — one end column, three candidate starts — and the tool should pick between them.
