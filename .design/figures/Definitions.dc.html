Table X. Three ways to read the same transcript
A contribution ends when the log says it concluded: a person's when they submit, the primary AI's when its turn is stamped complete, an agent's or a tool's when its result returns. That does not vary. When a contribution begins has three defensible answers, and each supports different questions.
Lens Shape A contribution starts A gap means Use it to see Cannot show
Record
the moment it ended almost everything is a gap exactly what was logged how long anything took
Work
when its author began nobody was working effort, idle time, concurrency each participant's share
Floor
when the previous one ended there are none who held the session, and how much idle time, and effort
Work is the default and what most analyses want. Record is the check to fall back on when a reading looks surprising and you need to know what was logged rather than derived. Floor is the reading closest to conversation analysis and the only one under which participation shares are meaningful, since it is the only one where the session sums to itself. Under Work, every row also carries whether its width was measured, estimated from length, or a placeholder for an act whose duration was never recorded, so a guess is never passed off as a measurement.
