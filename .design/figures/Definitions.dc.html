Table X. What counts as a start and an end, by participant
A contribution ends when the log states it concluded, and that is the same under every lens. Only the start is contested. The three columns are not competing estimates of one value: each measures a different mechanism, so each is correct for a different question.
Participant End Record Work Floor
Person Submit Equals the end. An instant. Reaches back over the composition window, where the text was composed rather than brought. From the previous participant's conclusion.
Primary AI Turn completion Equals the end. An instant. Reaches back by the recorded turn duration. From the person's submit.
Delegated agent Result returned Two instants: spawn and result. Spawn to result, where the delegation blocked. From spawn.
Tool Result returned Two instants: call and result. Call to result. From the caller's preceding act.
A gap means Nearly everything is a gap. Nobody was working. Genuine idle. Nothing. There are no gaps.
Each lens carries a different burden of evidence. Record claims only what was logged and is the conservative baseline. Work answers who was busy, and mixes measurement with inference, so it is reported alongside provenance. Floor answers whose turn it was, and is the reading closest to conversation analysis, being the only one under which the session is fully accounted for.
