Figure X. Most of what the person contributes was not written in the moment
A contribution can be composed at the keyboard or brought from elsewhere and pasted. The two are different interactional acts, and the record separates them: above roughly 120 words per minute the text cannot have been typed in the window available. In the multi-agent session, 2,632 of the person's 2,667 words arrived already written.

Two consequences follow. Any view that sizes a participant by word count reports brought text and authored text as one contribution, so the person's apparent share of an agentic session is largely material they carried in. And the tool-augmented session is the outlier in both directions: its human turns are short approvals genuinely typed at the keyboard, which is a different way of participating by the same person in the same week.
