Figure X. How much of each session is measured
Every contribution's position in time is observed. Its width is another matter. A turn carrying a recorded duration is measured; a message without one is estimated from its length; a tool call is given a fixed marker, because the log records that it happened but not how long it took. The share that is measured falls sharply as the interaction grows more agentic.

The chat session is half measured because the log emits one duration per human turn, and in a chat a human turn produces exactly one AI reply. In an agentic session a single turn produces a burst of messages and tool calls, and one duration still covers the lot. This is a property of the record rather than of the tool, and it is why a gap in the work lens is reported as unmeasured rather than as nothing having happened.
