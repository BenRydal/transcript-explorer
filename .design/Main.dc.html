What counts as a start and an end
Every contribution ends when the log says it concluded. Only the start is contested, and the three answers below are not competing guesses at one truth. Each measures a different mechanism, so each is correct for a different question.
Participant End (all lenses) Record — what was logged Work — who was busy Floor — whose turn it was
Person Submit moment Same as end. A tick. Composition window, where one exists. Otherwise a tick. From the previous participant's end.
Primary AI Turn completion stamp Same as end. A tick. Reaches back by the recorded turn_duration. From the person's submit.
Delegated agent Result returned Two ticks: spawn, result. Spawn to result, where the delegation blocked. From spawn.
Tool Result returned Two ticks: call, result. Call to result. From the caller's preceding act.
What a gap means Almost everything is a gap. Nobody was working. Genuine idle. Nothing. There are no gaps.
Record
Claims nothing the log did not state. The conservative baseline, and the one to fall back to when a reviewer asks what is observation and what is inference.
Work
Answers “who was busy, and for how long.” Mixes measurement with inference, so it needs the provenance shading to stay honest.
Floor
Answers “whose turn was it.” The closest to a conversation-analytic reading, and the only lens where the session is fully accounted for.
