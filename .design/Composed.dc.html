Why typing speed cannot be the answer
Most human words were never typed
Estimating the person's start from typing speed sounds right and breaks on the data. In the multi-agent session it is arithmetically impossible for 71% of human turns: the words could not have been typed in the window available. One turn of 1,497 words would need 4.99 million words per minute.
The explanation is not a bug
People paste. Syllabi, code, error output, URLs. Treating a paste as composition draws a bar reaching backwards through the previous participant's turn. But the ceiling that exposes the problem also turns it into a signal: a contribution the person composed and one they brought are different interactional acts, and the transcript can tell them apart.

What this does to the Speaker Garden
Bloom size encodes words. In the multi-agent session the person's bloom is built from 2,667 words, of which only 134 were composed in the moment. The visualization currently reports brought text and authored text as the same contribution.
Tool-augmented is the outlier, and that is the finding
Only 13% brought. Its human turns are short approvals genuinely typed in the moment. The same person, the same week, contributing in a categorically different way once tools absorbed the work.
